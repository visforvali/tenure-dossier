Overview

This appendix contains evidence of my service activity at the department, school, university, and public levels since joining Pace in 2018, in the form of relevant contracts, confirmations, certificates, emails, and other administrative materials. Although I have received small stipends or course releases in some of these roles, the labor required of me exceeds any compensation I am given; thus, I consider them to be part of my service activity. I have organized these materials categorically and chronologically.

For full descriptions of my service activity and philosophy, refer to my Service Statement, along with my Service Presentations, Impact Factor, Professional Development, and Reviews Conducted appendices.
