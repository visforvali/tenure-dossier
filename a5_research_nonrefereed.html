Overview

This appendix contains work I have published in nonrefereed venues since joining Pace in 2018, arranged in reverse chronological order. Although work outside of my probationary period (2020-25) does not factor into evaluation processes for tenure and promotion, I am including nonrefereed work produced as an NTT faculty member (2018-20) as an indicator of my broader research agenda and my dedication to remaining research active. Because many of these publications are OA webtexts, they are only accessible online. I have linked to them below and attached PDFs where feasible or necessary to do so, and I have also included PDFs of print-based publications.

For complete bibliographic information, descriptions, and any accolades received, please refer to my CV and Research Statement.
